# The-Magnetic-Tesla-Valve-for-Space-Radiation-Shielding
This is a theoretical study of a shield that can block solar radiation using the principle of a Tesla valve. Twelve artificial intelligence systems were used.
## Description(English)
Let's use a bit of imagination. Humans settling on other planets and the Moon will have to deal with a huge problem: radiation from the Sun. On Earth, it's not a problem because Earth's magnetic field protects us. So what will we do on the Moon and Mars? We can build an artificial magnetic shield, but it would consume unnecessary energy when the solar wind is low, and be insufficient when the solar wind is very strong. We need a shield that uses the energy of the solar wind itself. Thus, when the wind is weak, the shield is weak; when the wind is strong, the shield is strong. But it must not contain sensitive electronic parts, otherwise strong solar winds could fry them. How can we do it? Do you remember Tesla's valve? What is the main principle of that valve? Achieving one-way flow of water without using any mechanical parts. How? By using the force of the incoming water to create a counter-current that blocks the water from one direction. If water comes from the other direction, no counter-current forms. It has no moving parts that could break. So, can we use the incoming solar wind, directed in a way that creates a magnetic field, to make a shield?
