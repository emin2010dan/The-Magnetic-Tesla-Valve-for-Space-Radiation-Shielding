# Mathematical Simulation Roadmap for the Passive Plasma Dome Shield

## Overview

Before attempting a full Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) simulation, a staged approach is recommended.

Jumping directly into a large-scale plasma simulation would be computationally expensive and difficult to interpret.

Instead, the project can be divided into four progressive simulation levels.

---

# Simulation Level 1

## Feasibility Analysis

### Core Question

Can a relatively small seed magnetic field generate a significantly larger protective region?

### Solar Wind Dynamic Pressure

The dynamic pressure of the solar wind is:

$$
P_{sw} = n m_p v^2
$$

Where:

* $n$ = particle density
* $m_p$ = proton mass
* $v$ = solar wind velocity

---

### Magnetic Pressure

The magnetic pressure generated by the shield is:

$$
P_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}
$$

Where:

* $B$ = magnetic field strength
* $\mu_0$ = permeability of free space

---

### First-Order Equilibrium Condition

A first approximation of the shield boundary can be obtained by:

$$
P_{sw} \approx P_B
$$

This pressure balance defines the approximate location of the artificial magnetopause.

# Simulation Level 3

## Plasma Shell Formation

### Objective

Determine whether a self-organized plasma shell can form around the habitat.

This stage introduces Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD).

---

### Continuity Equation

$$
\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}
+
\nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{v})
==============================

0
$$

---

### Momentum Equation

$$
\rho
\left(
\frac{\partial \mathbf{v}}{\partial t}
+
\mathbf{v}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{v}
\right)
=======

-\nabla p
+
\mathbf{J}\times\mathbf{B}
$$

---

### Magnetic Induction Equation

$$
\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}
======================================

\nabla\times(\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B})
$$

---

### Desired Outputs

* Diamagnetic cavity formation
* Current sheet formation
* Plasma shell thickness
* Shield radius growth
* Plasma pressure maps

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Shield Gain

Defines how much larger the protective region becomes compared to the physical coil size.

$$
G =
\frac{R_{shield}}
{R_{coil}}
$$

Example:

* Coil radius = 1 m
* Shield radius = 50 m

$$
G = 50
$$

This may become the primary performance metric of the project.

---

## Radiation Reduction Factor (RRF)

Measures reduction of incoming particle flux.

$$
RRF =
1 -
\frac{\Phi_{inside}}
{\Phi_{outside}}
$$

Where:

* $\Phi_{inside}$ = flux reaching the habitat
* $\Phi_{outside}$ = incoming flux

Example:

$$
RRF = 0.70
$$

Meaning:

**70% reduction in charged particle exposure.**

# Central Scientific Question

The project ultimately seeks to answer:

> Can plasma-shell effects already observed in mini-magnetosphere research be enhanced through Tesla-valve-inspired asymmetric magnetic topologies?

If the answer is yes, the concept could evolve from a conventional magnetic shield into an entirely new research direction in lunar habitat protection.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The goal is not to overpower the solar wind.

The goal is to organize, redirect, and partially use the solar wind's own energy to build a larger protective structure around a habitat.

In its most ambitious form, the Passive Plasma Dome would become a self-scaling shield:

* stronger when solar activity increases,
* simpler than active shielding systems,
* and potentially robust enough for long-duration lunar settlements.
