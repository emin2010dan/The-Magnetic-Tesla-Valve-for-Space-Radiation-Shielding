Passive Solar Wind Magnetic Shield

A Tesla-valve-inspired shielding concept: solar wind plasma is guided into a toroidal current loop that generates its own magnetic field — stronger wind produces a stronger shield, with no active electronics.

② Step-by-step calculation
1 Kinetic energy flux
Φ = ½ · ρ · v_sw³ where ρ = n_sw · m_p
m_p = 1.67×10⁻²⁷ kg (proton mass). Power per unit area carried by the wind. Cube-law: doubling v_sw increases Φ by ×8.
2 Channel efficiency (Tesla principle)
η(θ) = sin²(θ/2)
The fraction of incoming plasma successfully redirected into the toroidal path. Peaks at θ = 90°–120° for practical designs. This is the direct analogue of the Tesla valve's geometric counter-current.
3 Effective ring current
I_eff = η · q · n_sw · v_sw · A_collector
q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C (proton charge). q · n_sw · v_sw is the charge flux [A/m²]. Multiplied by collector area gives total current. η scales how much of that current forms a closed toroidal loop.
4 Magnetic field (Biot-Savart)
B_shield = μ₀ · I_eff / (2R)
μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A. Field at the centre of a circular current loop of radius R. Larger R → weaker centre field but broader coverage — this is the key design trade-off.
5 Magnetopause distance
r_mp = R · [B² / (2μ₀ · ρ · v_sw²)]^(1/6)
The standoff distance where magnetic pressure equals solar wind dynamic pressure. Particles are deflected beyond this point. Target: r_mp ≥ 2 × r_habitat.

Deflection rate
D = 1 − exp(−r_mp / r_habitat)
D → 1: fully shielded  |  D → 0: unshielded. Target: r_mp ≥ 2 × r_habitat for D ≥ 86%.
Self-regulation scaling law
r_mp ∝ v_sw^(1/2)
Derived by combining all layers. Storm 4× faster → standoff 2× farther.
